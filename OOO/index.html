Out of Office

Finding balance between the lab and the world.

🏔️ Hiking

When the models are training, I’m usually off-grid. Hiking is my primary way to reset—there’s something rewarding about the iterative progress of a long ascent.

🍳 Kitchen Experiments

On holiday breaks I like treating my kitchen as another laboratory. Experimental bakes and pan assimilations are delightful pastimes.

⚽ Soccer

I have recently begun my soccer journey with above twenties ladies! Have been trying to get comfortable in my midfield and defense positions. Honestly, it's far from going well at the moment. But post match laughters and contemplations have been fun so far!

🎧 The Soundtrack
