Out of Office

Finding balance between the lab and the world.

🏔️ Hiking

When the models are training, I’m usually off-grid. Hiking is my primary way to reset—there’s something rewarding about the iterative progress of a long ascent.

🍳 Cooking

The kitchen is just another laboratory. I enjoy experimenting with spices and techniques—though the error bars in cooking are much more delicious.

⚽ Soccer

Strategy, speed, and teamwork. Whether I'm playing a match or catching a game, it’s a perfect exercise in real-time spatial optimization.

🎧 The Soundtrack

"You'll spot me at cafes drinking oat milk lattes, likely humming Radiohead way too often."

Music is the backbone of my focus. From the experimental textures of art-rock to the quiet atmosphere of a local Barnes & Noble, I find my best ideas where the rhythm is right.
